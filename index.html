02 / Two screens

Two screens. One pocket.

The top screen is where the world is. The bottom is where you live in it — maps, inventories, a second pair of eyes.

Games get to use both, because games used to. Nothing is scaled, nothing is stretched; a sprite drawn at 16 pixels arrives at 16 pixels. In daylight the LCD goes to 540 nits. At night it goes to 2.

04 / Play it now

Three buttons
is enough.

D-pad, A, B. That is the whole grammar. Pebble Garden — the tape in the box — runs right here on the same 60 Hz loop as the hardware.

Arrows / WASD walk · Space plant · R reset — or use the buttons

Walk onto a bare patch and press space to plant a seed. Press R to reset the garden. Twelve seeds finish the garden.
